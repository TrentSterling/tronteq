# Visualizer playback and render controls, September 24, 2026

**VIZ > Style > Playback & quality** now controls scene fades and rendering
resolution. Scene fade defaults to 0.65 seconds, applies to manual selections and
shuffle, and can be set from zero (instant) to two seconds. It follows elapsed
time independently of scene speed, including when a scene is held at speed zero.

Each transition captures the last composed artwork, including its effects and
brightness. Rapid selections begin from the image currently visible. The new
scene starts with clean feedback, while the outgoing image stays frozen during
the short fade. The old shader does not continue running. Turning scenes Off
clears transition state. Resizing the canvas or changing resolution cancels an
active fade while the render targets are rebuilt.

Resolution settings are **100%, 75%, and 50%** on each dimension. Only the artwork
and effects use this scale; EQ nodes, labels, and measurement overlays retain
native resolution. A GPU-time readout shows the rendered dimensions and averaged
GPU cost of the scene, effects, composition, and presentation. Timing uses three
queries and only reads available results; a busy GPU skips a timing sample rather
than making the UI wait.

Playback preferences persist in settings. Existing settings receive full
resolution and the default fade. Saved visual looks preserve the user's playback
preferences instead of changing them on recall. Sound settings are unaffected.
